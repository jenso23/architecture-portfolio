New Housing - Starkenburg Barracks

Residential Blocks & Communal Open Spaces

Typology Urban Planning & Zoning
Material Timber Construction & Perforated Clay Bricks
Location Darmstadt, Germany
Year 2024

As a part of the redevelopment of the Starkenburg Barracks in Darmstadt, an innovative neighborhood design is to be developed that creates high-quality and sustainable housing. This should meet the needs of future residents and foster appropriate interaction with the surrounding area.

The neighborhood concept includes the construction of three four-storey residential blocks, each consisting of three two-storey tower blocks, whose dimensions are adapted to the surrounding, small-scale structures of the forest colony. Targeted incisions and zoning create attractive interstices that offer both private and communal open spaces.
