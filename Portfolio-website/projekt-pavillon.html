Renovation of the Thannhausen Local History Museum

Timber Construction & Louvered Facade

Typology Renovation & Conversion
Material Timber Construction & Slats
Location Thannhausen, Germany
Year 2023

The task of my first independent project during my bachelor`s degree was the renovation and conversion of a small barn into a museum and event space for the village of Thannhausen in southern Germany.

The concept is to combine the historical with the modern. The barn retains its supporting structure, which is designed to be visible, but undergoes the necessary renovation. The ground floor remains solid, while the upper floor receives a new wooden louvre facade. These are arranged at different angles, providing sun protection at the openings and allowing diffused light into the rooms. From the outside, it appears as if there are no openings, preserving the building`s old charm.
